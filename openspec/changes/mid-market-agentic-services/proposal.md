# Change: Mid-market blue-collar agentic services

## Why

Founder Sam Desigan locked the ICP on 2026-09-13: Cubiczan sells **agentic services** to mid-market blue-collar / industrial operators with **$20M–$500M** revenue. The console still heroed sale-readiness, valuation bands, $1M-EBITDA lifestyle shops, and Anna/DoraHacks listing language.

## What changes

- User-facing copy (brand gate, skins, README, skills, listing files) sells diagnose → deploy agents → CHP lock → measured production
- Demo scenarios and challenge rules sit in $20M–$500M shop workflows (after-hours, job-cost, quote-to-cash)
- ServiceSell and FinBridge stay two go-to-market skins on one CHP spine
- Thumbnail / demo captions drop Anna App 276 and DoraHacks as the hero (small footer allowed)

## Out of scope

- Inventing named customers, emails, or Facebook URLs
- Splitting into two products or two CHP engines
- Replacing propose → challenge → lock, provenance, or the human lock
- Publishing to the Anna App Store
