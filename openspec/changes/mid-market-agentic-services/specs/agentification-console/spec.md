# Capability: agentification-console

## ADDED Requirements

### Mid-market agentic-services ICP

The brand gate and skin copy SHALL present Cubiczan agentic services for mid-market blue-collar / industrial operators with $20M–$500M revenue. The hero SHALL NOT sell sale-readiness, a valuation band, a fundraising pack, or a $1M-EBITDA lifestyle shop.

### Dual skins, one offer

ServiceSell SHALL be the field-service / trades skin (dispatch, booking, job-cost). FinBridge SHALL be the ops + cash skin (quote, job-cost, cash beyond the bookkeeper). Switching brand SHALL change theme and copy and SHALL NOT change tool ids.

### Shop-workflow demo books

Bundled scenarios SHALL use illustrative composites in the $20M–$500M revenue band and SHALL emphasize after-hours booking, job-cost, or quote-to-cash. Bundled scenarios SHALL NOT be presented as named customers.

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Dual brand, one product

The app SHALL expose ServiceSell and FinBridge as go-to-market skins of one Cubiczan agentic-services offer. Switching brand SHALL change theme and copy and SHALL NOT change tool ids.
