# Capability: agentification-console

## ADDED Requirements

### Workflow-agent ICP

The app SHALL present Cubiczan as helping blue-collar and field-service businesses implement AI agents in their live workflow. ServiceSell SHALL describe field-service agents (dispatch, after-hours booking, quoting, job-cost, field notes → invoice). FinBridge SHALL describe shop-floor finance-ops agents (quoting, job-cost, cash, after-hours ops). Neither skin SHALL present sale-readiness for institutional buyers or a valuation/raise pack as the primary promise.

### Shared ability, two skins

Both skins SHALL market the same ability. Switching brand SHALL change theme and copy only. Tool ids and CHP lock rules SHALL stay the same.

### No invented performance

User-facing copy SHALL NOT claim live ops metrics, reply rates, or named customers. Bundled scenarios SHALL remain illustrative composites.

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Dual brand, one product

The app SHALL expose ServiceSell and FinBridge as skins of one Cubiczan tool surface. Switching brand SHALL change theme and copy and SHALL NOT change tool ids.
