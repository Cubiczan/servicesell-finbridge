# Change: Blue-collar workflow agents

## Why

ServiceSell and FinBridge were marketing sale-readiness and a valuation/raise pack. Sam locked the product: Cubiczan helps blue-collar and field-service businesses implement AI agents in the live workflow. Both skins and the Anna App must sell the same ability.

## What changes

- User-facing copy (brands, UI, README, skills, judge paste, media titles)
- Sample books and challenge rules: after-hours capture, open quotes, job-cost leakage — not a buyer data-room
- Sale-only fields become workflow-agent fields; CHP propose → challenge → lock stays
- Thumbnail and demo titles no longer hero sale-readiness, valuation, or Anna App 276

## Out of scope

- Invented live ops metrics, reply rates, or named customers
- A second product or a second CHP engine
- Claiming DoraHacks / Anna App 276 as the customer pitch
